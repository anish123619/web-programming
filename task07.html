How to make kongguksu

A refreshig Korean noodle dish made with noodles and creamy soybean broth.

Ingredients

  • 200g Korean wheat noodles
  • 1 cup Cooked soybeans
  • 2 cups cold water
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Cucumber
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Soak and cook the soybeans until they become soft
  2. Blend the cooked soybeans with cold water until smooth.
  3. Add salt to the soybean broth according to your taste.
  4. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions
  5. Rinse the noodles with cold water and drain them well.
  6. Put the noodles into a bowl.
  7. Pour the cold soybean broth over the noodles.
  8. Add sliced cucumber and sesame seeds on top.
  9. Add a few ice cubes to make it extra cold

Serving

Serve the kongguksu cold and enjoy!
